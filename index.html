Day job

Performance analytics I build

Most of this runs on operating plants and stays internal. Happy to walk through the methods.

Irradiance QC

Onsite pyranometers checked against satellite references, with tracker deviation diagnostics for SAT, DAT and fixed tilt.

Loss attribution

Digital-twin waterfalls following IEC 61724-1: availability, clipping, curtailment, soiling and residual.

Soiling and cleaning

Soiling rates extracted from DC current and irradiance where no soiling station exists, and wash timing optimised against them.

Tracker diagnostics

Measured angles benchmarked against ideal backtracking, and stow detection from POA sensors alone.

Energy yield

Operational yield assessments with long-term climatology correction and Monte Carlo uncertainty (P50/P90).

SCADA pipelines

API extraction from monitoring platforms and historians, daily data-availability alerts and string-level exception reports.
